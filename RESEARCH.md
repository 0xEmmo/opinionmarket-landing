# OpinionMarket frontend rebuild research

## Requirements from OpinionMarketFrontendRedesignPlan.pdf

- Rework the current two-file prototype into one coherent, premium product frontend, not a marketing landing page.
- Use Polymarket and Kalshi as quality references for market discovery and information hierarchy without copying their branding or layouts.
- No emoji. Use deliberate vector iconography and Nigerian-relevant market imagery.
- Responsive sticky product header with brand, search, account action, and clearly marked demo wallet/balance.
- Interactive category rail with custom SVG icons, active-state movement, and touch-friendly horizontal scrolling.
- Featured market moment, search/sort controls, image-led cards with event/question, category, status, closing info, outcomes, trend and volume details.
- Trending, ending soon, and movers/market pulse context panels.
- Floating quick-stake button with swirl/radial-to-sheet interaction, reshuffle on repeat tap, and demo stake ticket. No real order, deposit, payment, or trading.
- Touch-first controls, keyboard focus, reduced motion, loading/empty/selected states.
- Visual direction: Nigerian market-floor/editorial feel; near-black/evergreen foundations, warm ivory data surfaces, bright green accent with a secondary market-signal color, typographic contrast, restrained motion.
- Event imagery should carry cultural energy; interface remains legible and financially credible.
- Avoid blue gradients, generic betting-template card grids, and a floating icon used only as a gimmick.
- Use one coherent market data model across filters, cards, side panels, and quick picks.
- Label illustrative/demo values and avoid unverified product, price, licensing, funding, or payout claims.

## Reference observations

OpinionMarket currently exposes a simple brand header, category links, featured sports/politics lists, flat all-market cards, and a footer with claims that are not appropriate to repeat in a demo. It lacks strong imagery, hierarchy, context modules, and a differentiated interaction signature.

Polymarket emphasizes browsable topic rails, featured event blocks, compact outcome rows, volume/deadline context, topic hubs, and a dense all-markets discovery feed. The useful pattern is information hierarchy, not its brand styling.

Kalshi emphasizes a clear exchange shell, trending topics, market/news highlight modules, forecast strips, popular markets, and movers. The useful pattern is activity/context around the primary market feed, not copying its layout.
